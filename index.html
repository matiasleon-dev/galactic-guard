GALACTICGUARD

Defending the Smart Lunar Building

Designing a secure and reliable network that protects a smart lunar building from cyber attacks and keeps communication with Earth safe.

EXPLORE MISSION →VIEW NETWORKS

OUR MISSION

Life on the Moon depends on connected technology. Our mission is to protect the systems that keep a smart lunar building operational and its communication with Earth secure.

◉

Air Systems

Life-support systems must remain available and protected.

♨

Temperature

Climate-control systems help protect people and equipment.

⌁

Communications

Critical voice, telemetry, video, and data links.

▣

Mission Data

Research and operational information must remain secure.

⚙

Control Systems

Connected building systems need reliable access controls.

⚠ THE PROBLEM

A cyber attack could disrupt critical lunar systems, compromise important data, or interfere with communication between the Moon and Earth.

WHY IT MATTERS

The smart lunar building depends on interconnected systems. Cybersecurity must be designed into the network from the beginning.

Keep it connected. Keep it secure. Keep it operational.

LUNAR CHALLENGES

Cybersecurity on the Moon must work with limited resources and a harsh environment.

☢

Radiation

Radiation can affect electronic equipment and system reliability.

ϟ

Limited Power

Security systems must be efficient with available energy.

⌁

Limited Bandwidth

Communication capacity between the Moon and Earth is limited.

◷

Communication Delays

Earth cannot instantly respond to everything happening on the Moon.

How do we build strong cybersecurity when power, bandwidth, hardware, and communication are limited?

OUR DEFENSE

A layered defense combines multiple controls so one failure does not expose the entire lunar building.

◆

Secure Networks

Keep systems separated and protected.

▦

Firewalls

Control what network traffic is allowed.

◉

Intrusion Detection

Monitor activity and alert to suspicious behavior.

▣

Encryption

Protect sensitive data across the network.

●

Access Control

Limit critical systems to authorized users and devices.
